# Audit Mesh MVP

## Goal

Build an AI-powered investigation platform for SMEs capable of identifying hidden financial patterns indicating potential micro-corruption.

## Success Criteria

The MVP is considered complete when a user can:

1. Upload financial data
2. Generate investigations automatically
3. Understand why transactions are suspicious (AI explanation)
4. View hidden relationships between entities
5. Produce investigation reports

Everything else is future scope.
